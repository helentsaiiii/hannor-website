One-Stop Admin Support

SET UP collaboration flow

From your first Taiwan office to the first local paycheck…

  1. 0

    Intake

    Intake call

    Clarify goals, entity options, headcount timing and constraints — then a Taiwan landing scope one-pager.

  2. 1

    Blueprint

    Diagnostic workshop

    A structured session that produces a landing blueprint and a clear RACI across client, Hannor and local partners.

  3. 2

    Registration

    Registration sprint

    Name check → document pack → filing → approval and tax ID — coordinated with your counsel where required.

  4. 3

    Compliance

    Policies & compliance base

    Core policies and regulatory housekeeping ready before the first joiners arrive.

  5. 4

    Payroll

    Payroll go-live / EOR bridge

    Local payroll or EOR bridge timed with hiring onboarding so day-one pay is covered.

  6. 5

    Handoff

    Stabilize & handoff

    Steady-state support through 1–3 payroll cycles, then a clean handoff — with a re-start window if you need us again.

How SET UP pairs with Map / Hire / Pay

  • Map — talent landscape and headcount plan inform which entity path and admin load you need.
  • Hire — registration and policies stay ahead of offer dates so candidates can start on schedule.
  • Pay — EOR or local payroll plugs in when the entity is ready, or bridges until it is.

Scope and timelines depend on your case and local requirements; formal filings and legal opinions are handled with qualified counsel as needed — this overview is not a legal guarantee.
